# Skarv Ventures — Fund II thesis (fictional)

We back Pre-Seed and Seed founders in the Nordics and DACH building software that
makes industry work better.

- **Industrial automation and robotics** — robots, machine vision and autonomy for
  factories, warehouses and logistics.
- **Climate and energy software** — grid, energy, carbon, battery and circularity tools.
- **Vertical B2B SaaS and AI** — workflow software for industry, supply chain,
  construction and manufacturing.

We do not invest in consumer apps, gaming, crypto or real estate.
